Which way the scale runs

Decision needed

The ATLAS 0.x design system reads the band as low to high uncertainty. The prototype repo reads it as a position from clearly outside to clearly within the DPS criteria. The marker, the caption and the sentence all depend on which one is chosen.

A · Amount of uncertainty
Lav usikkerhetHøy usikkerhet
ATLAS 0.x design system. Says how unsure, not which way.
B · Position against criteria
Klart utenforGråsoneKlart innenfor
Prototype repo. The end colours can read as a direction: calm sage to keep, warm clay to refer.
C · Position, grey zone marked (proposal)
Klart utenforGråsoneKlart innenfor
Keeps the repo’s meaning. Both ends are the same grey, so neither looks preferred; uncertainty is highest where the sand is.
RulesAppears instantly and never animates. No number, percentage or axis ticks. The caption is always present. The sentence describes; it never says what to do.
AccessibilityThe band is decorative to screen readers; the sentence is the accessible text. In forced-colours mode the track becomes a system-colour line and the marker stays.

Uncertainty types

Filled neutral tags that name what kind of uncertainty is present. Shown under the band; each opens a one-line definition.

Working definitions, to be confirmed by the clinical team.

Refer or hold

Two equal choices, nothing pre-selected, one optional rationale field, then a save button. Guardrails 2 and 3.

Din beslutning
Velg Behold eller Henvis for å lagre.
Beslutningen er lagret: {{ savedLabel }}.
RulesSame size, same style, fixed alphabetical order (Behold, Henvis) on every screen. The selected choice gets a brand outline and tint; the other stays as it was.
NeverA confirmation dialog, a warning colour, or an extra required field when the choice differs from where the band sits.

Summary and reasoning

The dual-process disclosure: a short summary for a quick read, and the full reasoning one click away. Built from Accordion.

{{ a.b }}
RulesThe summary is open by default; the reasoning is closed. Reasoning lists its sources (note, self-report, medication list) and what is missing.
