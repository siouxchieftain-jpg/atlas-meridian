Meridian design language · 1.0 for review

Design language

Meridian is the design language for ATLAS. On an atlas, a meridian is the line that joins the two poles; here it joins the GP and the DPS clinician on one record. ATLAS is built on Fluent 2, Microsoft’s design system, and adds the rules a clinical decision-support tool needs. Foundations, tokens and components follow Fluent 2 for web as published. Every colour is a Fluent token. ATLAS adds two things of its own: the uncertainty band, and a set of guardrails that stop the tool from recommending.

Design languagePrinciples, guardrails, colour, type, layout, shape, elevation, material, motion, icons, accessibility, content and tokens. ComponentsThe Fluent 2 components ATLAS uses, with ATLAS rules for each. Clinical patternsUncertainty band, refer or hold, dialogue with DPS, tours and the app shell.

Decisions in this version

  • Fluent 2 for web is the base. Neutral greys, the Segoe UI type ramp, 4px spacing, corner radius, shadows, motion and Fluent System Icons are used as published.
  • Every colour comes from Fluent’s global palette. The brand is Fluent lavender #7160E8, laid out as a 16-step brand ramp.
  • Kit: Fluent 2 Web, with the Fluent 2 design language tokens and Fluent System Icons. The other Microsoft kits are reviewed in Microsoft kits.
  • Warm paper, Source Serif 4, Inter and JetBrains Mono from ATLAS 0.x are retired.
  • Fluent’s status colours stay, for system feedback only. Clinical states are neutral.
  • The coloured wrapper panels (Register A) become guide surfaces with a dawn atmosphere: Fluent’s coral, pink and lavender tints. Clinical views stay white and grey, with lavender only for interaction; see Harmony.
  • Open: which way the grey-zone scale runs. See Clinical patterns.

Principles

Fluent 2 has four design principles. ATLAS keeps the structure and states what each one means in a consultation room and at a DPS intake desk.

01 · From “Natural on every platform”Natural in the clinicATLAS looks and behaves like the Windows and Microsoft 365 tools clinicians already use. Familiar controls leave attention for the patient.
02 · From “Built for focus”Built for clinical focusOne task per view. Neutral surfaces, with colour kept for meaning: lavender for interaction, the band for uncertainty. A consultation is 15–20 minutes; nothing competes for that time.
03 · From “One for all, all for one”One record, two rolesGP and DPS clinician see the same facts in the same components, identified by role. WCAG 2.2 AA throughout. Norwegian first, English on request.
04 · From “Unmistakably Microsoft”Honest about uncertaintyThe uncertainty band is the one element that is ATLAS’s own. It shows where a case sits and how unsure that placement is. It never says what to do.

Harmony

Each colour in ATLAS has one job, and each treatment belongs to one of two registers. Those two rules keep the atmosphere, the buttons and the band from competing for attention.

Lavender · interactionButtons, links, selection, focus, guide light.Never carries clinical meaning.

Sage, sand, clay · uncertaintyThe uncertainty band, and nothing else.Never in backgrounds, tags, charts or text.

Clinical colour: the uncertainty band

Sage, sand and clay carry equal weight; none is good or bad. They appear only inside the band, never on text, outcomes, badges or charts. They are too light to carry meaning alone (2.7, 1.7 and 3.8:1 on white), so the marker and a text label always do the work. They are the only colours not taken from Fluent. The nearest Fluent tokens are Brass tint 40 (#E0CEA2) for sand and Brown tint 20 (#A97652) for clay; Fluent has no muted sage.

Styling text

  • Sentence case in Norwegian and English. No all caps; the uppercase label style from ATLAS 0.x is retired.
  • Left-aligned. Centre only short text on guide surfaces and empty states.
  • Foreground 1 for content, Foreground 2 for secondary text, Foreground 3 for metadata and English lines. Brand colour only for links and selected states.
  • Tabular figures for times, doses and table columns.
  • Mono only for ICPC-2 and ICD-10 codes, IDs and timestamps.

  • No rounding where a surface meets the screen edge.

Elevation

Each Fluent shadow has two layers: a tight ambient shadow and a softer key shadow. Low elevations use 12% and 14% black; shadow 28 and 64 use 20% and 24%.

{{ s.t }}{{ s.u }}

ATLAS use

  • Clinical content stays at shadow 4 or below. Separation inside a panel comes from strokes and background steps, not shadows.
  • Higher shadows are for surfaces that come and go: menus, popovers, toasts, drawers, dialogs.
  • Cards that cannot be clicked do not lift on hover.
  • On brand-coloured surfaces, use Fluent’s brand shadows (30% ambient, 25% key) so elevation reads the same.

Material

Fluent has four materials: solid, mica, acrylic and smoke. ATLAS runs in a browser and shows clinical text, so it uses solid almost everywhere.

Solid · usedDefault for every surface. Mode-aware: white in light, #292929 in dark.
Mica · frame washThe app frame. Browsers cannot sample the desktop, so the frame uses atlasFrameWash, a faint gradient from neutral grey to brand 160.
Acrylic · guide surfaces onlyFrosted cards on the dawn atmosphere: white at 72% with a 24px blur. Never behind clinical text; menus and popovers in the app stay solid white.
Smoke · usedBackdrop for dialogs and overlay drawers. rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4) in light, 0.5 in dark.

Motion

Motion explains where something came from or went. Elements enter with deceleration and leave with acceleration. Larger elements get more time. ATLAS adds one rule: the uncertainty band never animates.

  • Colour changes on hover and press: faster (100ms), easyEase.
  • Menus, popovers and tooltips: fade and 10px slide, fast (150ms), decelerateMid.
  • Dialogs: fade in, gentle (250ms). Drawers: slide in, slow (300ms); out, normal (200ms) with accelerateMid.

Iconography

ATLAS uses Fluent System Icons (MIT licence). They replace the hand-drawn hairline set from ATLAS 0.x. Icons are named for what they show, not what they do.

  • 20px Regular is the default. 16px in dense tables and inline text, 24px on guide surfaces.
  • Filled marks the selected or active state: the current nav item, a checked toggle.
  • Colour: Foreground 2 by default; brand only when selected. No colour icons.
  • Clinical states always pair an icon with a text label.
  • Icon-only buttons need a tooltip and an accessible name.
  • No emoji anywhere.

Content design

ATLAS writes like a senior clinician writing a chart note for a colleague: precise, brief, never breezy. Fluent’s content rules apply: sentence case, short descriptive links, no directional words such as “above” or “to the right”.

Describe, never instructNo Anbefalt, Du bør or “Recommended”. The verb stays with the clinician. Buttons name the clinician’s own action: Lagre notat, Send til DPS.
Third person, no “we”The system addresses no one and speaks of itself only when needed. Pasienten beskriver …, not Du sier ….
Real terms, real timeUse the terms from the Helsedirektoratet priority guide. Referrals take days and triage takes weeks; never “instant” or “in seconds”.
